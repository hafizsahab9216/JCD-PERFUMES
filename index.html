Maison Heritage & Philosophy

The Alchemy of Invisible Luxury

At JCD PERFUMES, fragrance is not merely an accessory; it is an intimate aura, a liquid memory, and a declaration of sovereignty. Founded upon centuries-old distillation traditions, our atelier creates bespoke olfactory compositions designed specifically for discerning patrons across the globe.

Each formulation is created through patient aging and harmonious blending of sustainably sourced wild agarwood, Taif damask roses harvested at dawn, and rich ambers matured in French oak casks.

Rare Ingredients

Hand-harvested botanical extracts from Grasse, Mysore, Taif & Madagascar.

Artisanal Bottling

Hand-blown crystal flacons sealed with 24k gold leaf and signature wax.

The Olfactory Pyramid Structure

How our haute compositions evolve upon your skin through time.

1. Head (Top) Notes
First Impression: Calabrian Bergamot, Pink Pepper, Sicilian Mandarin, Cardamom Pods.
First 15 - 30 Minutes
2. Heart (Middle) Notes
The Core Identity: Taif Damask Rose, Royal Jasmine Sambac, Honeyed Amber, Saffron.
2 - 6 Hours Evolution
3. Base (Dry-Down) Notes
The Enduring Signature: Cambodian Oud, Mysore Sandalwood, Madagascan Vanilla, Ambergris.
12 - 24+ Hours Longevity
